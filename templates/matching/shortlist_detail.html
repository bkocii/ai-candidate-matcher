Inspectable scoring

How the score is calculated

Each must-have skill has two weight units and each nice-to-have skill has one. The combined weights are normalized to exactly 100 points, so an individual must-have is worth approximately twice an individual nice-to-have. Missing skill evidence earns no points but is not treated as proof that the candidate lacks the skill.

Ranking order: score descending, then passed before needs-review only when scores tie, then stable record ID. Explicit hard-constraint failures are excluded regardless of score.
