# Future Backlog

These ideas are explicitly outside the MVP and require separate approval.

- Authorized ATS integrations such as customer-approved Greenhouse or Lever access.
- Approved external candidate-source connectors.
- Email integration with exact-recipient verification and explicit send approval.
- Multi-tenant hosted SaaS architecture.
- Subscription billing.
- Candidate self-service consent, correction, and deletion portal.
- Calendar and interview scheduling.
- Team comments, assignments, and service-level reminders.
- Embedding-based candidate retrieval backed by a persistent vector database.
- Feedback-assisted ranking with bias and quality evaluation.
- Multiple AI providers and customer-selected models.
- OCR for explicitly approved scanned-image CVs, with equivalent resource limits
  and privacy controls.
- Localization, including Albanian.
- Windows desktop edition reusing application-domain services.
- Mobile-friendly recruiter workflow or dedicated mobile app.
- Production ATS replacement capabilities.

No backlog item should be pulled into an active sprint merely because it appears easy.
